22.【解析】(1) $X$ 的概率密度函数为 $f(x)=\begin{cases}\dfrac{1}{\theta}, & 0<x<\theta, \\ 0, & \text{其他},\end{cases}$ 则 $X$ 的分布函数为

$$
F(x)=\begin{cases}0, & x<0, \\ \dfrac{1}{\theta}x, & 0\leqslant x<\theta, \\ 1, & x\geqslant\theta.\end{cases}
$$

$X_{(n)}$ 的分布函数为
$$
\begin{aligned}
F_{X_{(n)}}(x)&=P\left\{\max\{X_1,X_2,\cdots,X_n\}\leqslant x\right\}=P\left\{X_1\leqslant x,X_2\leqslant x,\cdots,X_n\leqslant x\right\} \\
&=F^n(x)=\begin{cases}0, & x<0, \\ \dfrac{1}{\theta^n}x^n, & 0\leqslant x<\theta, \\ 1, & x\geqslant\theta.\end{cases}
\end{aligned}
$$

$X_{(n)}$ 的概率密度函数为
$$
f_{X_{(n)}}(x)=\begin{cases}\dfrac{n}{\theta^n}x^{n-1}, & 0<x<\theta, \\ 0, & \text{其他},\end{cases}
$$

$$
\therefore E(T_c)=cE(X_{(n)})=c\int_0^\theta x\cdot\dfrac{n}{\theta^n}x^{n-1}\mathrm{d}x=\dfrac{cn}{n+1}\theta=\theta,\text{得 }c=\dfrac{n+1}{n}.
$$

(2) $E(T_c^2)=c^2E(X_{(n)}^2)=c^2\int_0^\theta x^2\cdot\dfrac{n}{\theta^n}x^{n-1}\mathrm{d}x=\dfrac{c^2n}{n+2}\theta^2,$

$$
\begin{aligned}
h(c)&=E(T_c-\theta)^2=E(T_c^2-2\theta T_c+\theta^2)=E(T_c^2)-2\theta E(T_c)+\theta^2 \\
&=\dfrac{c^2n}{n+2}\theta^2-2\dfrac{cn}{n+1}\theta^2+\theta^2,
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
h'(c)=\dfrac{2cn}{n+2}\theta^2-2\dfrac{n}{n+1}\theta^2=0,\text{得 }c=\dfrac{n+2}{n+1},\text{而 }h''(c)=\dfrac{2n}{n+2}\theta^2>0,
$$

$$
\therefore c=\dfrac{n+2}{n+1}\text{ 时},h(c)\text{ 取最小值}.
$$
