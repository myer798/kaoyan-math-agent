由于随机变量 $X_1, X_2, \cdots, X_n$ 独立同分布，故 $X_1^2, X_2^2, \cdots, X_n^2$ 相互独立。

$$
D\left(\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}X_i^2\right) = \frac{1}{n^2}D\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n}X_i^2\right) = \frac{1}{n^2}\sum_{i=1}^{n}D(X_i^2) = \frac{1}{n^2}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left\{E(X_i^4)-[E(X_i^2)]^2\right\}
$$

$$
= \frac{1}{n^2} \cdot n(\mu_4 - \mu_2^2) = \frac{\mu_4 - \mu_2^2}{n}.
$$

代入(1)式可得，

$$
P\left\{\left|\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}X_i^2 - \mu_2\right| \geq \varepsilon\right\} \leq \frac{\mu_4 - \mu_2^2}{n\varepsilon^2}.
$$

因此，应选 A.
