【答案】$-\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{8}$

【解析】直接求导可得
$$
\dfrac{dy}{dx} = \dfrac{dy/dt}{dx/dt} = \dfrac{1 - \sin t}{4\sin t\cos t} = \dfrac{1 - \sin t}{2\sin 2t}
$$
$$
\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2} = \dfrac{d}{dt}\left(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\right)\bigg/\dfrac{dx}{dt} = \dfrac{-\cos t\sin 2t - 2\cos 2t(1 - \sin t)}{4\sin^3 2t}
$$
于是$\left.\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}\right|_{t=\frac{\pi}{4}} = -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{8}$

14. $\displaystyle\int_1^{+\infty}\dfrac{\ln(x+1)}{x^2}dx =$__________

【答案】$2\ln 2$

【解析】分部积分可得
$$
\int_1^{+\infty}\dfrac{\ln(x+1)}{x^2}dx = -\int_1^{+\infty}\ln(x+1)d\left(\dfrac{1}{x}\right) = -\left.\dfrac{\ln(x+1)}{x}\right|_1^{+\infty} + \int_1^{+\infty}\dfrac{1}{x(x+1)}dx = \ln 2 + \left.\ln\dfrac{x}{x+1}\right|_1^{+\infty} = 2\ln 2
$$

15. 设矩阵 $A = \begin{pmatrix}1 & 0 & 0 \\ 2 & a & 2 \\ 0 & 2 & a\end{pmatrix}$，$B = \begin{pmatrix}a & -1 & -1 \\ -1 & 2 & 1 \\ -1 & -1 & a\end{pmatrix}$，$m(X)$ 表示矩阵 $X$ 的最大特征值，且 $m(A) < m(B)$，则 $a$ 的取值范围是__________

【答案】$a < 0$

【解析】
$$
\begin{vmatrix}
\lambda - 1 & 0 & 0 \\
-2 & \lambda - a & -2 \\
0 & -2 & \lambda - a
\end{vmatrix}
= (\lambda - 1)\left[(\lambda - a)^2 - 4\right] = (\lambda - 1)(\lambda - a - 2)(\lambda - a + 2)
$$
$$
\therefore \lambda_1 = 1,\, \lambda_2 = a + 2,\, \lambda_3 = a - 2
$$
$$
\begin{vmatrix}
\lambda - a & 1 & 1 \\
1 & \lambda - 2 & -1 \\
1 & 1 & \lambda - a
\end{vmatrix}
\overset{r_1 \to r_1 - r_3}{=}
\begin{vmatrix}
\lambda - a - 1 & 0 & 1 - \lambda + a \\
1 & \lambda - 2 & -1 \\
1 & 1 & \lambda - a
\end{vmatrix}
\overset{c_3 \to c_3 + c_1}{=}
\begin{vmatrix}
\lambda - a - 1 & 0 & 0 \\
1 & \lambda - 2 & 0 \\
1 & 1 & \lambda - a + 1
\end{vmatrix}
$$
$$
= (\lambda - a - 1)(\lambda - a + 1)(\lambda - 2)
$$
$$
\therefore \lambda_1 = 2,\, \lambda_2 = a + 1,\, \lambda_3 = a - 1
$$

① 当 $a + 2 > 1$ 时，$m(A) = a + 2$，此时 $a > -1$，$a + 1 > 0$

又 $a + 2 > a + 1 > a - 1$，要使 $m(A) < m(B)$，$m(B) = 2$，此时 $2 > a + 2 \Rightarrow a < 0$ 矛盾
