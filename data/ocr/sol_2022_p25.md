设 $x_1, x_2, \cdots, x_n, y_1, y_2, \cdots, y_m$ 是相应于样本 $X_1, X_2, \cdots, X_n, Y_1, Y_2, \cdots, Y_m$ 的一组样本值，则似然函数

$$
L(\theta) =
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{1}{2^m \theta^{m+n}} e^{-\dfrac{\sum\limits_{i=1}^n x_i}{\theta} - \dfrac{\sum\limits_{j=1}^m y_j}{2\theta}}, & x_i > 0, y_j > 0, i = 1,2,\cdots,n, j = 1,2,\cdots,m, \\
0, & \text{其他}.
\end{cases}
$$

当 $x_i > 0, y_j > 0, i = 1,2,\cdots,n, j = 1,2,\cdots,m$ 时，取对数得

$$
\ln L(\theta) = -m\ln 2 - (m+n)\ln\theta - \dfrac{\sum\limits_{i=1}^n x_i}{\theta} - \dfrac{\sum\limits_{j=1}^m y_j}{2\theta}.
$$

令 $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}[\ln L(\theta)]}{\mathrm{d}\theta} = 0$，即 $-\dfrac{m+n}{\theta} + \dfrac{\sum\limits_{i=1}^n x_i}{\theta^2} + \dfrac{\sum\limits_{j=1}^m y_j}{2\theta^2} = 0$，解得 $\theta = \dfrac{2\sum\limits_{i=1}^n x_i + \sum\limits_{j=1}^m y_j}{2(m+n)}$。

因此，$\theta$ 的最大似然估计量 $\hat{\theta} = \dfrac{2\sum\limits_{i=1}^n X_i + \sum\limits_{j=1}^m Y_j}{2(m+n)}$。

下面计算 $D(\hat{\theta})$。

$$
\begin{aligned}
D(\hat{\theta}) &= D\left[ \dfrac{2\sum\limits_{i=1}^n X_i + \sum\limits_{j=1}^m Y_j}{2(m+n)} \right] = \dfrac{1}{(m+n)^2} D\left( \sum\limits_{i=1}^n X_i \right) + \dfrac{1}{4(m+n)^2} D\left( \sum\limits_{j=1}^m Y_j \right) \\
&= \dfrac{1}{(m+n)^2} \sum\limits_{i=1}^n D(X_i) + \dfrac{1}{4(m+n)^2} \sum\limits_{j=1}^m D(Y_j) = \dfrac{nD(X)}{(m+n)^2} + \dfrac{mD(Y)}{4(m+n)^2} \\
&= \dfrac{n\theta^2}{(m+n)^2} + \dfrac{m \cdot 4\theta^2}{4(m+n)^2} = \dfrac{\theta^2}{m+n}.
\end{aligned}
$$

或者，注意到 $\hat{\theta} = \dfrac{2\sum\limits_{i=1}^n X_i + \sum\limits_{j=1}^m Y_j}{2(m+n)} = \dfrac{2n\overline{X} + m\overline{Y}}{2(m+n)}$，利用 $D(\overline{X}) = \dfrac{D(X)}{n}, D(\overline{Y}) = \dfrac{D(Y)}{m}$ 可得，

$$
\begin{aligned}
D(\hat{\theta}) &= D\left[ \dfrac{2n\overline{X} + m\overline{Y}}{2(m+n)} \right] = \dfrac{n^2 D(\overline{X})}{(m+n)^2} + \dfrac{m^2 D(\overline{Y})}{4(m+n)^2} = \dfrac{n^2}{(m+n)^2} \cdot \dfrac{D(X)}{n} + \dfrac{m^2}{4(m+n)^2} \cdot \dfrac{D(Y)}{m} \\
&= \dfrac{n\theta^2}{(m+n)^2} + \dfrac{m \cdot 4\theta^2}{4(m+n)^2} = \dfrac{\theta^2}{m+n}.
\end{aligned}
$$
