$$
\therefore \left.\frac{\mathrm{d}^{2} y}{\mathrm{~d} x^{2}}\right|_{x=0}=-3+2 \times 4=5.

13.【答案】$-\frac{1}{\pi}$

【解析】将 $f(x)$ 偶延拓，再进行周期延拓，则有
$$
\begin{aligned}
a_{n} & =\frac{2}{\pi} \int_{0}^{\pi} f(x) \cos n x \mathrm{~d} x=\frac{2}{\pi} \int_{0}^{\pi}(x+1) \cos n x \mathrm{~d} x=\frac{2}{\pi} \cdot \frac{1}{n} \int_{0}^{\pi}(x+1) \mathrm{d} \sin n x \\
& =\frac{2}{n \pi}\left[(x+1) \sin n x\left|_{0}^{\pi}-\int_{0}^{\pi} \sin n x \mathrm{~d} x\right]\right.=-\frac{2}{n \pi} \int_{0}^{\pi} \sin n x \mathrm{~d} x=\left.\frac{2}{n^{2} \pi} \cos n x\right|_{0} ^{\pi} \\
& =\frac{2}{n^{2} \pi}\left[(-1)^{n}-1\right].
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\therefore \lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} n^{2} \sin a_{2 n-1}=\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} \frac{n^{2} 2 \cdot(-2)}{(2 n-1)^{2} \pi}=-\frac{1}{\pi}.
$$

14.【答案】$y=\tan \left(y+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)-x$

【解析】令 $u=x+y, y=u-x, \frac{\mathrm{d} y}{\mathrm{~d} x}=\frac{\mathrm{d} u}{\mathrm{~d} x}-1$, 代入原方程得
$$
\frac{\mathrm{d} u}{\mathrm{~d} x}=\frac{1+u^{2}}{u^{2}}, \text { 即 } \frac{u^{2}}{1+u^{2}} \mathrm{~d} u=\mathrm{d} x,
$$
两边积分得
$$
u-\arctan u=x+C,
$$
即
$$
x+y-\arctan (x+y)=x+C,
$$
由 $y(1)=0$, 得 $C=-\frac{\pi}{4}, \therefore y=\arctan (x+y)-\frac{\pi}{4}$, 即
$$
x+y=\tan \left(y+\frac{\pi}{4}\right).
$$

15.【答案】$a \geqslant 0$

【解析】由 $\left(\alpha^{\top} A \beta\right)^{2} \leqslant \alpha^{\top} A \alpha \beta^{\top} A \beta$, 得 $\alpha^{\top} A\left(\beta \alpha^{\top}-\alpha \beta^{\top}\right) A \beta \leqslant 0$ 恒成立，
$$
\begin{aligned}
\beta \alpha^{\top}-\alpha \beta^{\top} & =\left(\begin{array}{l}
y_{1} \\
y_{2}
\end{array}\right)\left(x_{1} \quad x_{2}\right)-\left(\begin{array}{l}
x_{1} \\
x_{2}
\end{array}\right)\left(y_{1} \quad y_{2}\right)=\left(\begin{array}{cc}
0 & x_{2} y_{1}-x_{1} y_{2} \\
x_{1} y_{2}-x_{2} y_{1} & 0
\end{array}\right) \\
& =\left(x_{2} y_{1}-x_{1} y_{2}\right)\left(\begin{array}{ll}
0 & 1 \\
-1 & 0
\end{array}\right).
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
A\left(\beta \alpha^{\top}-\alpha \beta^{\top}\right) A & =\left(x_{2} y_{1}-x_{1} y_{2}\right)\left(\begin{array}{cc}
a+1 & a \\
a & a
\end{array}\right)\left(\begin{array}{ll}
0 & 1 \\
-1 & 0
\end{array}\right)\left(\begin{array}{cc}
a+1 & a \\
a & a
\end{array}\right) \\
& =a\left(x_{2} y_{1}-x_{1} y_{2}\right)\left(\begin{array}{ll}
0 & 1 \\
-1 & 0
\end{array}\right).
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\therefore \alpha^{\top} A\left(\beta \alpha^{\top}-\alpha \beta^{\top}\right) A \beta & =a\left(x_{2} y_{1}-x_{1} y_{2}\right)\left(x_{1} \quad x_{2}\right)\left(\begin{array}{ll}
0 & 1 \\
-1 & 0
\end{array}\right)\left(\begin{array}{l}
y_{1} \\
y_{2}
\end{array}\right) \\
& =-a\left(x_{1} y_{2}-x_{2} y_{1}\right)^{2} \leqslant 0 \text { 恒成立, }
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\therefore a \geqslant 0.
