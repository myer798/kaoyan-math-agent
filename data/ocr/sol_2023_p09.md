两式相减得：

$$
f(a) - f(-a) = \frac{1}{2} \left[ f''(\eta_1)(a - x_0)^2 - f''(\eta_2)(a + x_0)^2 \right]
$$

又因为 $|f''(x)|$ 连续，设 $M = \max\{|f''(\eta_1)|, |f''(\eta_2)|\}$，故

$$
|f(a) - f(-a)| \leq \frac{1}{2} \left[ |f''(\eta_1)|(a - x_0)^2 + |f''(\eta_2)|(a + x_0)^2 \right]
$$
$$
\leq \frac{1}{2} M \left[ (a - x_0)^2 + (a + x_0)^2 \right] = M(a^2 + x_0^2) \leq 2Ma^2
$$

所以：$M \geq \frac{1}{2a^2}|f(a) - f(-a)|$，故存在 $\eta = \eta_1$ 或 $\eta = \eta_2 \in (-a, a)$，使得

$$
|f''(\eta)| \geq \frac{1}{2a^2}|f(a) - f(-a)|.
$$

21.（本题满分 12 分）

已知二次型

$$
f(x_1, x_2, x_3) = x_1^2 + 2x_2^2 + 2x_3^2 + 2x_1x_2 - 2x_1x_3, \quad g(y_1, y_2, y_3) = y_1^2 + y_2^2 + y_3^2 + 2y_2y_3.
$$

(1) 求可逆线性变换 $x = Py$，将 $f(x_1, x_2, x_3)$ 化成 $g(y_1, y_2, y_3)$；

(2) 是否存在正交变换 $x = Qy$，将 $f(x_1, x_2, x_3)$ 化成 $g(y_1, y_2, y_3)$？

解．(1) 由配方法得：

$$
f(x_1, x_2, x_3) = x_1^2 + 2x_2^2 + 2x_3^2 + 2x_1x_2 - 2x_1x_3 = (x_1 + x_2 - x_3)^2 + (x_2 + x_3)^2
$$

令
$$
\begin{cases}
z_1 = x_1 + x_2 - x_3 \\
z_2 = x_2 + x_3 \\
z_3 = x_3
\end{cases}
$$
，则 $f(x_1, x_2, x_3) = z_1^2 + z_2^2$。再由配方法得：

$$
g(y_1, y_2, y_3) = y_1^2 + y_2^2 + y_3^2 + 2y_2y_3 = y_1^2 + (y_2 + y_3)^2
$$

令
$$
\begin{cases}
z_1 = y_1 \\
z_2 = y_2 + y_3 \\
z_3 = y_3
\end{cases}
$$
，则 $g(y_1, y_2, y_3) = z_1^2 + z_2^2$。于是

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
z_1 \\ z_2 \\ z_3
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 1 & -1 \\
0 & 1 & 1 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
x_1 \\ x_2 \\ x_3
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 1 & 1 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
y_1 \\ y_2 \\ y_3
\end{pmatrix}
$$

从而
$$
P =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 1 & -1 \\
0 & 1 & 1 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{pmatrix}^{-1}
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 1 & 1 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & -1 & 1 \\
0 & 1 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{pmatrix}.
