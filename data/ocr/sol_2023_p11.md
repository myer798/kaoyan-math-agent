当 $0 \leq x < 1$ 时，

$$
F_Z(z) = P\left\{x^2 + y^2 \leq z\right\} = \iint\limits_{x^2 + y^2 \leq z} \frac{2}{\pi}(x^2 + y^2)\,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y
$$

$$
= \int_0^{2\pi} \mathrm{d}\theta \int_0^{\sqrt{z}} \frac{2}{\pi}r^2 \cdot r\,\mathrm{d}r = z^2.
$$

于是：

$$
f_Z(z) = 
\begin{cases}
2z, & 0 \leq z < 1 \\
0, & \text{其他}
\end{cases}.
$$
