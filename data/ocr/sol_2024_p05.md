16.【答案】$\frac{2}{3}$

【解析】设随机变量 $X$ 表示三次独立重复实验中成功的次数，则 $X \sim B(3, p)$，  
$$
P\{X = k\} = C_3^k p^k (1 - p)^{3-k},\ k = 0,1,2,3,
$$
则 $\frac{4}{13} = P\{X = 3 \mid X \geqslant 1\} = \frac{P\{X = 3, X \geqslant 1\}}{P\{X \geqslant 1\}} = \frac{P\{X = 3\}}{1 - P\{X = 0\}} = \frac{p^3}{1 - (1 - p)^3}$，解得 $p = \frac{2}{3}$。

17.【解析】区域 $D$ 关于 $x$ 轴对称，记 $D$ 位于 $x$ 轴上方区域为 $D_1$，  
则  
$$
\iint_D \frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}} \,\mathrm{d}x\,\mathrm{d}y = 2\iint_{D_1} \frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}} \,\mathrm{d}x\,\mathrm{d}y,
$$
记  
$$
D_2 = \{(x, y) \mid 0 \leqslant x \leqslant 1, 0 \leqslant y \leqslant 1\},\quad D_3 = \{(x, y) \mid x^2 + y^2 \leqslant 1, x \geqslant 0, y \geqslant 0\},
$$
则  
$$
\iint_{D_1} \frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}} \,\mathrm{d}x\,\mathrm{d}y = \iint_{D_2} \frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}} \,\mathrm{d}x\,\mathrm{d}y - \iint_{D_3} \frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}} \,\mathrm{d}x\,\mathrm{d}y
$$
$$
= \int_0^1 \mathrm{d}y \int_0^1 \frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}} \,\mathrm{d}x - \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \mathrm{d}\theta \int_0^1 r\cos\theta \,\mathrm{d}r
$$
$$
= \int_0^1 \sqrt{1 + y^2} \,\mathrm{d}y - \int_0^1 y \,\mathrm{d}y - \frac{1}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} + \frac{1}{2}\ln(1 + \sqrt{2}) - \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{2},
$$
$$
\therefore \iint_D \frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}} \,\mathrm{d}x\,\mathrm{d}y = \sqrt{2} + \ln(1 + \sqrt{2}) - 2.
$$

18.【解析】（1）$\left.\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\right|_{(1,1)} = \left[3x^2 - 2(x + y)\right]\bigg|_{(1,1)} = -1$，  
$$
\left.\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\right|_{(1,1)} = \left[3y^2 - 2(x + y)\right]\bigg|_{(1,1)} = -1,
$$
$\therefore$ 曲面 $z = f(x, y)$ 在 $(1,1,1)$ 处的切平面法向量为 $n = (-1, -1, -1)$，  
$\therefore$ 切平面 $T$ 方程为 $-(x - 1) - (y - 1) - (z - 1) = 0$，即 $x + y + z - 3 = 0$。

$$
